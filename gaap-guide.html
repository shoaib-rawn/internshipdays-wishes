QUICKBOOK ERP BACKEND · ACCOUNTING GUIDE

Accounting rules,
in simple words.

This page explains some of the transaction behavior documented in my API. The Swagger page has the full endpoints and examples.

A quick note

This is a guide to my project’s documented behavior, not accounting advice or a claim that the software is fully GAAP-compliant. Actual reporting requirements should be checked with a qualified accountant.

01

Chart of accounts

Accounts organize the company’s financial records. Set up the required accounts before using them in items and transactions.

CHART OF ACCOUNTS
02

Estimates

An estimate is a quote. Creating one does not change account balances.

ESTIMATES
03

Invoices

A posted invoice increases Accounts Receivable and the amount due from the customer.

INVOICES
04

Customer payments

Payments reduce linked invoice and customer balances. The amount applied to invoices cannot exceed the payment total.

PAYMENTS
05

Sales receipts and credit memos

A sales receipt records an immediate sale without creating Accounts Receivable. A credit memo reduces the customer’s open balance.

SALES RECEIPTS · CREDIT MEMOS
06

Purchases and bills

Purchase orders do not post balances in the documented workflow. Vendor bills increase payables; bill payments settle linked bills.

PURCHASE ORDERS · BILLS · BILL PAYMENTS
07

Keeping a record

Some delete operations reverse balances or retain a record in the audit trail. Invoices can also be voided.

REVERSALS · AUDIT TRAIL
